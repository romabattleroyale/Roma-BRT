ROMA ROYALE — V7 DOOR CLEARANCE STATIC AUDIT

This is a geometry/code audit, not a runtime Godot test.

Rules checked:
- Main entrance centered on the front facade.
- Noble palaces: wall opening 2.25 m, door 2.05 m.
- Standard/commercial entrances: wall opening 1.45–1.55 m, door 1.25 m.
- Front bugnato skips the entrance corridor.
- Noble/portico columns leave the center bay clear.
- Ground-floor header is raised to 2.60 m so tall doors are not cut by the wall.
- Small commercial buildings use a centered entrance opening.

Audited archetypes: 24.
Minimum lateral opening clearance: 0.20 m.
Portico center bay: clear of columns for Palazzetto Romano, Palazzo Nobile and Palazzo con Portico.

Runtime note:
Godot 4.7 was not available in this tool environment, so the project was not launched here. Please verify visually on the phone.
